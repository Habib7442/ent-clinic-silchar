# Project Overview: The ENT Clinic, Silchar

## 1. Description
A premium, high-performance, bilingual (English & Bengali) website for a specialist ENT clinic in Silchar, Assam. The project aims to convert a Google Maps-only presence into a defensible digital asset that serves the Cachar, Karimganj, and Hailakandi regions (~2.1M population).

## 2. Goals
1.  **Establish Trust:** Signal expertise through clinical transparency, real photography, and published credentials.
2.  **Drive Conversions:** Direct patients into a frictionless 1-tap booking flow (Call, WhatsApp, or Online Slot Picker).
3.  **Local SEO Dominance:** Win the local 3-pack and rank #1 for high-intent symptom queries in both English and Bengali.
4.  **Performance Excellence:** Achieve a 95+ Lighthouse score on mid-tier Android devices on 4G.
5.  **Bilingual Reach:** Unlock the regional search universe by providing high-quality Bengali content.

## 3. Core User Flow
1.  **Entry:** User arrives via Google Search (Local Pack or Symptom Query) or direct referral.
2.  **Recognition:** User immediately recognizes their symptom or need on the Home or Condition page.
3.  **Trust Building:** User scrolls through doctor credentials, clinic photos, and real patient reviews.
4.  **Resolve:** User sees transparent pricing and "What to Expect" info, lowering anxiety.
5.  **Conversion:** User books a slot via WhatsApp, Phone, or the Online Calendar in maximum 3 taps.
6.  **Confirmation:** User receives a WhatsApp/SMS confirmation with instructions and an .ics calendar file.

## 4. Features (MVP)
### Patient-Facing
- **Bilingual Support:** Full site in both English and Bengali.
- **Integrated Booking:** WhatsApp Business API, Tap-to-Call, and Slot Picker.
- **Service Hub:** Six specialized service pages (Hearing, Sinus, Snoring, Pediatric, Voice, Surgery).
- **Condition Microsites:** 12 SEO-optimized pages for specific symptoms.
- **Transparent Pricing:** Published consultation fees.
- **Doctor Profile:** Editorial-style profile with NMC credentials.
- **Location & Access:** Custom map with drive-time and accessibility info.

### Content & SEO
- **Journal Hub:** Editorial blog for long-tail SEO.
- **Schema Implementation:** Rich results for MedicalClinic, Physician, and FAQ.
- **Review Integration:** Live Google Reviews with reply visibility.

## 5. In-Scope
- 14 core pages + 12 condition microsites.
- English and Bengali hand-translated content.
- WhatsApp Business API integration.
- Next.js 15 frontend with Sanity.io CMS.
- Performance optimization for local connectivity.

## 6. Out-of-Scope
- Complex patient portal or EHR integration (MVP focuses on booking only).
- Online payments for all treatments (Consultation fee payment optional).
- In-house audiology testing software (referrals only).
- Mobile app development.

## 7. Success Criteria
- [ ] 95+ Lighthouse score across all 4 metrics.
- [ ] Booking flow completed in under 3 taps from any page.
- [ ] First booking received within 14 days of go-live.
- [ ] #1 ranking for "ENT doctor Silchar" and "Ear pain doctor Silchar".
- [ ] Zero stock photos used in the final build.
